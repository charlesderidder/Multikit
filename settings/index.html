Browser settings

Activate or restore browser access here. Configure the app in your web browser.

Choose a username and password of at least 6 characters for browser access.

HTTP is not encrypted. Use only a trusted local network; do not expose this port to the internet.

http://HOMEY_IP:3000/multikit-settings/

Replace HOMEY_IP with your Homey's local IP address. If you have changed the port, restart the app to apply it.

Virtual devices and modes are added from Homey's Devices screen under Multikit. The browser settings page explains their pairing options, icons and Flow capabilities.
